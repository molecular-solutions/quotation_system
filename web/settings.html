Settings

Defaults used for every new quotation.

Academic numbers run in one shared sequence; Commercial numbers are typed on the quote. Changing the next sequence number takes effect on the next Academic quote. Company address, logo and the standard terms printed on the document come from the Word template (templates/master_quotation.docx).

E-mail recipients

The Email button on a quotation sends the PDF to these addresses. One per line (or separated by commas).

This server has no outgoing mail set up yet: add QUOTE_SMTP_HOST, QUOTE_SMTP_USER, QUOTE_SMTP_PASSWORD and QUOTE_SMTP_FROM to .env and restart (see the README).

Terms & Conditions — the points printed on page 2 of every quotation

One point per box. Formatting: **bold** __italic__ ==highlight==. Placeholders filled in per quotation: {validity_date} {payment_terms} {license_term}. E-mail addresses turn blue automatically. Quotations already issued keep the terms they were created with.
